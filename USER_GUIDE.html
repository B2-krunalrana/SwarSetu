SwarSetu

Desktop App & Mobile Streaming Guide

How SwarSetu Works

SwarSetu sets up a secure, ultra-low latency real-time voice bridge. Audio recorded on your phone is transmitted instantly via secure WebSockets to your PC.

  • Local Secure Connection: Bypasses external cloud networks completely, running entirely over local Wi-Fi.
  • Automated SSL (HTTPS): Programmatically creates local certificates so mobile browsers grant microphone access safely.
  • High-Fidelity Audio: Streams 16 kHz Mono PCM audio chunks directly to your PC speakers.

Interactive Setup Wizard Guide

Step 1 First Launch Only

When you double-click SwarSetu.exe for the first time, you are presented with a premium branding page explaining what the app does.

1 Click action to proceed:

  • Look for the cyber-cyan button labeled Get Started ➔ in the middle of the frame.
  • Click it to transition to the Requirements page.
Step 2 Security Checklist

This screen lists requirements: Local firewall access (Port 8000), SSL HTTPS context creation, and Audio output speaker access.

1 Toggle agreement box:

  • Locate the checkbox at the bottom: ✓ "I agree to grant these permissions."
  • Click the checkbox to check it. This will unlock the proceed button.

2 Click to continue:

  • Click the newly enabled Continue ➔ button to launch the initialization sequence.
Step 3 Automatic Initialization

The loader screen initializes the system. You do not need to click anything here. The app automatically completes the following tasks:

✓ Automated Actions:

  • 🔒 SSL Certificates: Generates self-signed `cert.pem` and `key.pem` files in the folder.
  • 🔊 Speaker Scan: Queries local audio drivers for available output systems.
  • 🚀 Server Startup: Boots the FastAPI server on port 8000 in a background thread.

*Once all three systems are loaded, the wizard completes and automatically transitions you to the Live Dashboard in 2 seconds.

Step 4 Active Connection Mode

The main control dashboard is displayed. On all future launches of the application, SwarSetu will skip the wizard and open this dashboard directly.

1 Connect your Phone:

  • Connect your phone and PC to the **same Wi-Fi network**.
  • Scan the **QR Code** on the screen with your phone's camera, or click 📋 Copy and send the link to your phone.

2 Stream Voice Announcements:

  • Open the link on your phone (tap **Advanced -> Proceed** if your browser warns about the self-signed certificate).
  • Tap the green **"Allow Mic"** button on the web page.
  • Speak! The audio will stream directly out of your PC speakers. Adjust the **Speaker Selection Dropdown** in the PC app if needed.

Troubleshooting & Tips

Browser shows "Secure Origin Required" or fails to play?
Mobile browsers block microphone access unless the page is loaded over HTTPS. SwarSetu generates SSL certificates automatically. Ensure you accept the self-signed certificate exception on your phone's browser when navigating to the address.
How do I run the Wizard again?
The setup completion state is stored in your user profile. To force the Setup Wizard to run again on launch, simply delete the file: ~/.swarsetu_config.json in your user home directory.
How do I update the speaker?
Use the dropdown selector labeled "Output Speaker Device" at the bottom of the Live Dashboard. If you plug in a new speaker, click the blue **"Refresh"** button to reload the devices.
How do I exit the application?
Closing the window (X button) at any stage (Wizard or Dashboard) immediately terminates the background FastAPI server and exits the program safely.
